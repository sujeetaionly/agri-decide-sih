IDEA TITLE
❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
🌾 Detailed Explanation
  • Zero-Friction Decision Support: Replaces static Kaggle N-P-K classification with dynamic agro-climatic & economic intelligence.
  • Automated GIS Integration: Auto-fetches baseline soil texture (SoilGrids) and historical climate GDD via GPS/Taluka without requiring lab soil test kits.
  • Multi-Crop Comparison: Evaluates candidate crops side-by-side and ranks by Net Realization per Day (₹/Day).
  • Crop Rotation Incentive: Rewards nitrogen-fixing legume rotations after cereals to sustain long-term soil fertility.
🎯 Target: Smallholder Farmers (< 2 Hectares)
🎯 How it Addresses Problem
  • Soil & Water Reality: Directly factors farmer's irrigation type (borewell, canal, open well, rainfed) and capacity.
  • Sowing Window Validation: Evaluates planned sowing dates against regional ICAR cutoffs, penalizing delayed sowing yields.
  • True Economic Viability: Ingests official Ministry CACP cost norms and dynamically deducts machinery rental if owned (tractor/sprayer).
  • Market Specialization: Respects regional crop clustering (APMC mandis, labor, seed ecosystems).
💧 Solves: Water, Sowing & Budget Mismatch
💡 Innovation & Uniqueness
  • Explainable AI Reasoning: Clear, farmer-friendly bullet points explaining why the top crop is recommended.
  • Interactive "What-If" Sandbox: Live sliders allowing evaluators/farmers to simulate climate shocks (-20% rain) and sowing delay (+15d).
  • Rural Voice Accessibility: Dual-language PWA (English/Hindi) with hands-free crop voice input (🎤 बोलकर बताएं) and TTS audio.
  • 120-Day Action Timeline: Sowing-to-harvest milestone schedule for fertilizers, irrigation, and mandi tracking.
🎙️ Accessibility: Hindi/English Voice PWA
