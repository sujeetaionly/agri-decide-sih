IDEA TITLE
❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
• The Solution
  • Android app: farmer answers 5 simple cards (farm size, soil photo, water, past crop, sowing date) → ranked crop comparison with full ₹/Acre economics in < 90s.
  • Uses hyper-local district data — nearest APMC mandi prices, taluka SoilGrids GIS soil, district climate normals — not national averages.
• How It Solves the Problem
  • Zero lab testing — auto-fetches soil by GPS. Uses official CACP A₂+FL cost norms, deducts ₹3,500 if farmer owns tractor.
  • Crop rotation intelligence — penalizes monoculture (−15%), rewards cereal→legume (+12%) based on previous crop history.
• What Makes It Unique
  • 🆚 Head-to-Head: Farmer selects intended crop → system compares against AI pick with exact ₹ and % profit delta.
  • Explainable AI: Tells farmer WHY in Hindi — "काली मिट्टी में सोयाबीन को अधिक नमी मिलती है".
  • BHASHINI Voice: MeitY Digital India speech for hands-free rural access.
