PROPOSED SOLUTION
❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
🌾 Detailed Explanation of Proposed Solution
  • Hyper-Local Taluka Intelligence: Replaces static national averages with live GPS-adaptive SoilGrids soil properties, nearest APMC mandi prices, and IMD climatology.
  • 6-Card Intuitive Intake Wizard: Captures farm acreage, visual DSLR soil cards, water sources, previous crops, sowing timing, and intended crops in < 90 seconds.
  • Auto Season & Window Detection: Automatically classifies Kharif/Rabi/Zaid and filters strictly for viable regional crop varieties.
🎯 How it Addresses the Core Problem
  • Eliminates Chemical Lab Test Barrier: Auto-fetches soil texture and pH via GIS — requires zero chemical N-P-K testing by smallholders.
  • Crop Rotation Intelligence: Enforces biological sustainability by penalizing monoculture (-15%) and rewarding cereal→legume rotations (+12%).
  • CACP Operational Economics: Ingests official Ministry $A_2+FL$ cost norms and dynamically deducts machinery rental if owned (saving ₹3,500–₹4,300/ac).
💡 Innovation & Uniqueness
  • 🆚 Head-to-Head Comparison: Directly pits farmer's intended crop against AI-recommended champion with exact ₹ profit difference and % gain.
  • Net Profit per Acre (₹/Acre): Ranks recommendations by true seasonal net realization.
  • Digital India BHASHINI Voice AI: Multilingual speech-to-text (ASR) + natural audio narration in 10 Indic languages.
📊 Live Android Scorecard: Net ₹/Acre Ranking, CACP Cost Deduction & Head-to-Head Profit Comparison
