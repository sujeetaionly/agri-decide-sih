❖ IDEA / PROPOSED SOLUTION :
Fasal-Disha gives farmers clear financial foresight before sowing. Instead of relying on guesswork or routine, it delivers field-specific yield forecasts, realistic crop cost breakdowns, and live risk simulation.
❖ Digital Soil Health Mapping: Reads soil pH, texture, and organic carbon directly from GIS SoilGrids without waiting weeks for chemical lab test reports.
❖ Smart Crop Rotation Guidance: Scientifically rewards legume crop rotations that restore soil nitrogen while warning against repetitive monoculture soil depletion.
❖ Sowing Window Optimization: Recommends ideal sowing dates and calculates calibrated daily yield loss penalties if monsoon or planting is delayed.
❖ Hyper-Local Mandi Sync: Detects major crops grown locally in the region and syncs 5-year APMC mandi auction rates with local input costs to forecast realistic harvest profits.
❖ Problem Resolution :
❖ Focuses on In-Hand Net Profit: Moves farmers away from just chasing gross harvest volume to knowing their true pocket profit (₹/Acre) after deducting all seeds, fertilizer, and labor costs.
❖ Restores Degraded Soil: Breaks harmful single-crop habits through multi-season rotation plans that naturally preserve nitrogen and organic soil health.
❖ De-Risks Weather & Market Shocks: Lets farmers test "what if monsoon falls short" or "what if mandi prices crash" before investing hard-earned money in inputs.
❖ Village-Level Advisory Precision: Replaces generic state-level averages with tailored guidance matching the farmer's exact village, soil type, water source, and owned machinery.
❖ Unique Value Propositions (UVP) :
❖ Side-by-Side Crop Comparison: Shows the exact profit delta (₹/Acre) comparing multiple crops the farmer is thinking of sowing against the AI recommendation.
❖ Live "What-If" Risk Sliders: Interactive sliders allowing farmers to test how rainfall amounts or market price swings impact their expected profit in real time.
❖ Farmer-Owned Machinery Savings: Automatically deducts cost savings if the farmer owns a tractor, pump, or thresher rather than assuming expensive rental rates.
❖ 100% Spoken Voice Guidance: Full speech recognition and audio playback in Hindi, Marathi, and regional languages for seamless adoption by any farmer.
❖ Offline Printable Slip & WhatsApp: 1-Click vector PDF advisory slip and instant WhatsApp sharing with family and local agricultural extension officers.
