IDEA TITLE
❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)
• Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
  • Hyper-Local Taluka Intelligence: Delivers crop economics using real taluka data — nearest APMC mandi wholesale prices, SoilGrids GIS soil texture, and IMD normals.
  • 6-Card Intake Wizard: Captures farm size, DSLR soil photo, water source, previous crop, sowing timing, and intended crop in < 90 seconds.
  • Auto Season & Crop Filtering: Classifies Kharif/Rabi/Zaid and filters dynamically for regionally validated crop varieties.
• How it addresses the problem
  • Eliminates Chemical Lab Test Barrier: Auto-fetches soil texture and pH via GPS GIS — requires zero chemical N-P-K testing by smallholder farmers.
  • Crop Rotation Intelligence: Enforces biological sustainability by penalizing monoculture (-15%) and rewarding cereal→legume rotations (+12%).
  • CACP Operational Economics: Ingests official Ministry $A_2+FL$ cost norms and dynamically deducts machinery rental if owned (saving ₹3,500–₹4,300/ac).
• Innovation and uniqueness of the solution
  • 🆚 Head-to-Head Comparison: Directly pits farmer's intended crop against AI-recommended champion with exact ₹ profit difference and % gain.
  • Net Profit per Acre (₹/Acre): Ranks recommendations by true seasonal net realization.
  • Digital India BHASHINI Voice AI: Multilingual speech-to-text (ASR) + natural audio narration in 10 Indic languages for rural low-literacy farmers.
